01 Eligibility

You must be 18 years or older and a resident of Nigeria (or physically present in Nigeria for the duration of the hunt) to register as a Hunter. By registering, you confirm that the details you provide — name, age, state, email, and WhatsApp number — are accurate and belong to you.

Employees, organizers, and immediate family members of The Last Haunter 9ja team are not eligible to win prizes but may participate for fun.

02 Entry Fee & Payment

Entry into each hunt costs ₦2,000, payable via the payment options listed on the registration page at the time of entry (which may include Paystack, OPay, or Moniepoint, or bank transfer).

All entry fees are strictly non-refundable once payment is confirmed, regardless of whether you go on to participate, win, or complete the hunt.

If you pay via bank transfer, transfer link, or any manual method, your slot is only confirmed once your transaction reference has been verified by the organizing team. Unverified or unmatched payments will not be registered.

03 Hunter Code & Registration

Upon successful payment and verification, you will receive a unique Hunter Code via WhatsApp or email within 24 hours. This code identifies you for the duration of the hunt and is required to submit clue answers.

Your Hunter Code is personal and non-transferable. Sharing your code with another person to submit on your behalf may result in disqualification of both parties.

04 Gameplay & Clues

Clues are released weekly starting from the official hunt launch date. Each clue may direct Hunters to a physical location, a social media post, or a specific piece of content to identify (such as a name, brand, or song).

  • Hunters are responsible for their own safety, transportation, and conduct while completing any physical or location-based clues.
  • Clues must be solved using publicly available information. Attempting to obtain answers directly from organizers, staff, or other Hunters through improper means is prohibited.
  • The organizing team's interpretation of a clue and its correct answer is final.

05 Submissions & Deadlines

Each clue has a stated submission window and closing time. Submissions received after the deadline will not be counted, regardless of the reason for the delay (network issues, travel, etc.).

Where a clue requires photo or video proof, the media must clearly and genuinely demonstrate that you personally completed the task. Reused, staged with someone else's footage, or digitally altered submissions will be disqualified.

06 Prizes & Winner Verification

Prize amounts and structure for the current hunt are published on the main hunt page and may vary between hunts. Winners are determined by accuracy and speed of correct submissions across all clues, as tracked by the organizing team.

Before prize payout, winners must verify their identity (matching the name used at registration) and may be asked to provide a valid means of identification. Prizes will be paid directly to the winner's bank account or mobile wallet within a reasonable period after verification.

The Last Haunter 9ja reserves the right to withhold or reassign a prize if fraud, rule violation, or false information is discovered at any stage.

07 Code of Conduct & Disqualification

Hunters are expected to behave respectfully toward organizers, other Hunters, and members of the public encountered during the hunt. The following will result in immediate disqualification without refund:

  • Providing false registration information
  • Sharing or selling your Hunter Code
  • Harassment, trespassing, or unsafe/illegal conduct while completing a clue
  • Submitting fraudulent, staged, or plagiarized proof
  • Colluding with other Hunters to falsely inflate scores or bypass hunt rules

08 Privacy & Data Use

Information collected at registration (name, age, state, email, WhatsApp number, and payment reference) is used solely to manage your participation — confirming entry, sending clues, verifying submissions, and contacting winners. It will not be sold to third parties.

By submitting photo or video proof, you grant The Last Haunter 9ja permission to feature that content on official social media pages for promotional purposes, with credit to you where reasonably possible.

09 Liability & Safety

Participation in physical or location-based clues is entirely at your own risk. The Last Haunter 9ja is not liable for any injury, loss, theft, or damage incurred while completing a clue. Hunters should exercise reasonable caution, travel in groups where possible, and avoid unsafe locations or situations.

The organizing team reserves the right to cancel, postpone, or modify any hunt, clue, or deadline due to circumstances beyond its control (e.g. safety concerns, platform outages, or force majeure), and will communicate any such changes to registered Hunters.

10 Changes to These Terms

These Terms & Conditions may be updated between hunts. The version in effect at the time you register is the version that applies to your entry. Continued participation after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
