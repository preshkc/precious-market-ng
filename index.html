Questions

QUICK ANSWERS

Do I need to be in Lagos to play?

No. This hunt is 100% remote. You can play from anywhere in Nigeria or abroad — all you need is a smartphone and internet connection.

How do I receive my prize?

Prize money is transferred directly to your Nigerian bank account within 60 minutes of the winner announcement on the live stream.

Can I play in a team?

Each entry is individual. However, teams are allowed — just enter separately and decide internally how to split prize money. One code, one winner name.

How are winners verified?

The first correct submission with all answers in the right order wins. We verify each answer against source content with timestamps before announcing.

What if I miss the start time?

You can join any time during the hunt window (usually 2–3 hours). However, the hunt ends when a correct submission is received — so speed matters.

Is this a lottery or gambling?

No. This is a skill and knowledge competition. Winners are determined by accuracy and speed, not chance. It is fully legal under Nigerian law.

How do I become a member?

Members get bonus clue hints, early registration, discounted entry and exclusive content. Membership starts at ₦1,500/month — link in bio.

What platforms do I need accounts on?

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook are the core platforms. Wikipedia and Google are also common. All are free and publicly accessible.
